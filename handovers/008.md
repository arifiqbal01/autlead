1. Project Overview
Autlead is a self-hosted, modular B2B lead acquisition, enrichment, and outreach system written in Python.
Its job is to:
- discover companies from external sources;
- normalize and deduplicate them;
- persist authoritative company/source data in PostgreSQL;
- enrich persisted companies using website, technology, performance, contact, people, and email-verification capabilities;
- later select suitable enriched companies for campaigns and outreach;
- support multiple products such as WebArtsy and Autply without duplicating the underlying data-processing infrastructure.
The project uses a modular monolith + provider architecture. External capabilities such as Google Maps discovery, Crawl4AI, technology detection, email verification, Groq/LLMs, and Resend are replaceable providers. Autlead owns orchestration, persistence, policies, and product decisions.
The most important current architectural simplification is:
Acquisition
    ↓
PostgreSQL
    ↓
Enrichment
    ↓
PostgreSQL
    ↓
Outreach

These are three independent generic Autlead flows, not WebArtsy-specific pipelines.
PostgreSQL is the durable source of truth and boundary between flows.

5. Decisions Log
Decision — 2026-09: Replace product-specific operational pipelines with three generic flows
Older design centered around things such as:
pipelines/webartsy/
pipelines/autply/

Operational ETL/enrichment should instead be generic:
acquisition
enrichment
outreach

Why:
WebArtsy and Autply use the same fundamental company discovery/enrichment infrastructure. Product differences mainly occur in:
ICP
signals
scoring
qualification
intelligence
messaging
outreach strategy

Duplicating extraction/enrichment per product would cause drift and unnecessary maintenance.
What breaks if reversed:
Shared stages become duplicated, provider integrations become product-coupled, and fixing enrichment behavior requires modifying multiple product pipelines.
Status: active decision.
Decision — Acquisition stops after persistence
Acquisition's responsibility ends after company/source persistence.
Why:
It creates a clean durable boundary and makes acquisition independently runnable.
Alternative rejected:
One continuous pipeline from Maps discovery through crawling, people, email, and outreach.
Why rejected:
It couples expensive downstream work to discovery and makes resume/reprocessing difficult.
Decision — Enrichment operates only on persisted companies
Enrichment does not perform discovery.
Why:
Companies can be re-enriched without rediscovery, providers can change independently, and enrichment failures do not destroy acquisition work.
Decision — Outreach is independent
Outreach consumes persisted enriched companies.
Why:
Sending email is a higher-impact operation and should not be an automatic side effect of enrichment.
Decision — no pipelines/common/ for pipeline-specific logic
Earlier code contained:
app/pipelines/common/contacts.py

Exception:

The caller/lifecycle owns transaction boundaries.
Decision — homepage/business pages remain special rehydrating stages
These cannot behave exactly like ordinary checkpointed stages.
Reason:
Even if their enrichment checkpoint says completed, downstream contacts/people processing may still require WebsiteContent.
Therefore a completed checkpoint cannot simply return None.
They may load persisted content or refresh it depending on crawl retention.
Do not blindly replace these with a generic run_checkpointed_stage() implementation.
Decision — website crawl freshness is independent from enrichment checkpoint completion
A company may have:
enrichment stage = completed

while the persisted website crawl is old enough to require refresh.
Therefore:
COMPLETED crawl
    ↓
retention expires
    ↓
RUNNING
    ↓
COMPLETED

is valid.
WebsiteCrawlStatus.COMPLETED is not terminal forever.
The state transition table must include:
WebsiteCrawlStatus.COMPLETED: {
    WebsiteCrawlStatus.RUNNING,
},

This was proven necessary by an actual runtime failure.
Decision — people stage merges old orchestration + deterministic core
People enrichment currently performs:
pages
    ↓
deterministic extraction
    ↓
optional Groq refinement
    ↓
decision-maker policy classification
    ↓
deterministic normalization/entity resolution/ranking

Person / PersonObservation persistence

The orchestration function is:

Do not split the old WebArtsy wrapper back out merely for architectural symmetry.
Decision — Groq failure falls back to deterministic people
If Groq refinement fails, deterministic candidates remain usable.
This prevents an LLM outage/rate limit from destroying otherwise useful extraction.
The fallback historically catches GroqProviderError and converts deterministic candidates into low-confidence/unknown AI-refinement representations before continuing.

10. People Stage Details
People processing behavior that must be preserved:
1. Extract people deterministically from every relevant website page.
2. Refine candidates once per company with Groq.
3. If Groq fails, use deterministic candidates.
4. Apply decision-maker policy/classification.
5. Run deterministic normalization/entity resolution/ranking.
6. Persist Person.
7. Persist PersonObservation.
8. Caller owns transaction.
Normalization includes roughly:
" ".join(name.split()).casefold()

Persistence records observation time using UTC.
The merged people stage currently uses the checkpoint:
EnrichmentStage.PERSON_PERSISTENCE

not PEOPLE.
This is intentional because the old company orchestration checkpointed the merged persisted result under "person_persistence".
Do not casually switch it to "people_analysis" or historical checkpoint behavior will change.
11. Person Email Stage Details

15. Important New Finding — Groq Payload / Rate Limit
During the single-company run:
people extracted = 1

but Groq refinement failed.
The provider reported:
TPM limit: 8000
Requested: 9659

The system retried three times before falling back to deterministic candidates.   Pasted text
This is an important scaling problem.
The fallback worked, so the company still succeeded.
However, retrying an inherently oversized request wastes approximately tens of seconds per affected company.
Required follow-up
Before large enrichment batches:
1. Bound the website/page content passed to Groq.
2. Ensure the constructed request fits provider limits.
3. Distinguish a retryable transient rate limit from a request that is intrinsically too large.
4. Do not repeatedly retry an unchanged payload that can never fit.
Do not solve this by removing the deterministic fallback.

28. Known Issues / Landmines
Do not run thousands of companies yet
The single-company pipeline works, but Groq and person-email behavior need validation first.
Do not remove deterministic Groq fallback
Groq already hit a real rate/token problem.
The fallback allowed enrichment to succeed.
Do not simply increase Groq retries
The observed request itself was larger than the available TPM limit.
Retrying the unchanged request is not a real solution.
Bound/reduce the request.
Do not assume person-email's four people are a bug
They may be legitimate persisted historical people.
Inspect DB/query semantics first.
Do not change PERSON_PERSISTENCE checkpoint casually
Merged people processing currently uses:
EnrichmentStage.PERSON_PERSISTENCE

Do not rename persisted stage strings
Keep:
website_analysis
performance_seo
person_persistence
...

unless doing an intentional migration.
Do not rename/remove WebArtsyStageState just because it looks stale
It is currently the persistence model.
A generic Python API wraps it.
Changing it is a separate migration/refactor.
Do not turn homepage/business pages into ordinary skip-only checkpoints
Downstream stages need page content.
They must rehydrate persisted data when appropriate.
Do not share AsyncSession across concurrent company workers

       └──────────┬──────────┘

The design goal is not architectural sophistication.
The design goal is:
Explicit, resumable, provider-independent flows that reliably move real company data through acquisition, enrichment, and eventually outreach while PostgreSQL remains authoritative.

The project is currently at the point where acquisition works and generic enrichment has completed a real single-company end-to-end run. The next work is not another large refactor: it is to validate persisted-person selection, fix Groq payload sizing, then prove enrichment on a small multi-company batch before building the minimal outreach/campaign flow.
